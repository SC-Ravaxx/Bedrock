STAR CITIZEN

Bedrock is an unofficial tool. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated
with Cloud Imperium Games or Roberts Space Industries. "Star Citizen",
"Squadron 42", "Roberts Space Industries" and "Cloud Imperium" are trademarks
of Cloud Imperium Rights LLC.

Bedrock reads the game the way a person does: it looks at the screen and at
the game's own Game.log. It does not read the game's memory, attach to its
process or inject anything into it. One optional feature writes a language
file into the game folder, the way language packs do.
